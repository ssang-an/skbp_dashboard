## 2. Criterion Details

### Target Relevance

- Score: 3/3
- Evidence Type: E3_asset_specific_preclinical_or_technical_evidence
- Reason: Alzheimer's disease is an SKBP interest indication, and ILM01 directly links Aβ clearance to glial TAM-receptor neuroimmune biology.
- Evidence type reason: Alzheimer's disease is an SKBP interest indication, and ILM01 directly links Aβ clearance to glial TAM-receptor neuroimmune biology.
- Why not higher: 3 is the maximum score.
- Uncertain points:
- The exact intended clinical stage and biomarker-defined population have not been publicly finalized.
- The relative contribution of Aβ binding versus TAM-driven immune modulation in humans is unknown.

### Competitive Landscape

- Score: 2/3
- Evidence Type: E3_asset_specific_preclinical_or_technical_evidence
- Reason: ILM01 shows quantitative preclinical differentiation from reference anti-Aβ antibodies, but it will enter a crowded field with two marketed and several Phase 2/3 Aβ biologics.
- Evidence type reason: ILM01 shows quantitative preclinical differentiation from reference anti-Aβ antibodies, but it will enter a crowded field with two marketed and several Phase 2/3 Aβ biologics.
- Why not higher: A score of 3 is not supported because high-similarity Aβ biologics are numerous and ILM01 lacks clinical head-to-head evidence or a verified leading position.
- Uncertain points:
- The identity of the deck's reference antibody is not consistently disclosed across experiments.
- No clinical comparison of ARIA, cognition, dosing burden, or brain exposure is available.

### MoA Validity

- Score: 3/3
- Evidence Type: E3_asset_specific_preclinical_or_technical_evidence
- Reason: Asset-specific TAM engagement, Aβ uptake, cytokine modulation, and in vivo inflammatory-marker changes provide direct functional validation of the proposed mechanism.
- Evidence type reason: Asset-specific TAM engagement, Aβ uptake, cytokine modulation, and in vivo inflammatory-marker changes provide direct functional validation of the proposed mechanism.
- Why not higher: 3 is the maximum score.
- Uncertain points:
- Public materials do not show full receptor-selectivity, occupancy, or causal blockade datasets for the final ILM01 candidate.
- Human translation of the inflammation-silent mechanism remains untested.

### Platform Attractiveness

- Score: 2/3
- Evidence Type: E3_asset_specific_preclinical_or_technical_evidence
- Reason: GAIA is a reusable binder-swappable platform with quantitative engineering gains, including a reported greater-than-30-fold productivity improvement.
- Evidence type reason: GAIA is a reusable binder-swappable platform with quantitative engineering gains, including a reported greater-than-30-fold productivity improvement.
- Why not higher: No platform-derived asset has reached first-patient-dosed status, and repeated quantitative technical advantage across multiple platform assets has not been publicly demonstrated.
- Uncertain points:
- The greater-than-30-fold productivity claim is company-generated and lacks a public methods package.
- Cross-asset manufacturability and pharmacology data are not public.

### Expansion Potential

- Score: 0/3
- Evidence Type: E0_not_found_or_not_assessable
- Reason: No additional indication is confirmed as an active ILM01-specific program beyond Alzheimer's disease.
- Evidence type reason: No additional indication is confirmed as an active ILM01-specific program beyond Alzheimer's disease.
- Why not higher: Platform-level target expansion and separate assets such as ILM02 or ILM21 do not count as ILM01 indication expansion.
- Uncertain points:
- No ILM01-specific efficacy, PD, or biomarker data were found in a second indication.

### Data Maturity

- Score: 3/3
- Evidence Type: E3_asset_specific_preclinical_or_technical_evidence
- Reason: ILM01 has multiple complementary quantitative domains: in vitro mechanism, human-cell translational models, mouse PK, in vivo efficacy/inflammation, and ex vivo human AD tissue.
- Evidence type reason: ILM01 has multiple complementary quantitative domains: in vitro mechanism, human-cell translational models, mouse PK, in vivo efficacy/inflammation, and ex vivo human AD tissue.
- Why not higher: 3 is the maximum score; the absence of human data is captured as a stage and diligence risk rather than lowering a rubric-compliant preclinical maturity score.
- Uncertain points:
- Some key numerical data appear only in the company deck and have not been independently replicated.
- The relationship between the 2022 predecessor construct and the final ILM01 lead is not fully disclosed.

### Marketability

- Score: 2/3
- Evidence Type: E2_indirect_or_class_level_evidence
- Reason: A conservative U.S.-based calculation gives estimated global obtainable peak sales of $1,289.68 million, supporting a score of 2.
- Evidence type reason: A conservative U.S.-based calculation gives estimated global obtainable peak sales of $1,289.68 million, supporting a score of 2.
- Why not higher: The calculated global value is below $2,000 million, and no reliable asset-specific external peak-sales forecast or clinical differentiation supports a higher value.
- Uncertain points:
- Public net price is unavailable; Leqembi WAC is used as a proxy.
- Eligibility, access, penetration, and competition haircut are analyst assumptions.
- Human dosing frequency, treatment duration, efficacy, safety, and launch timing are unknown.

## 6. Final Recommendation

ILM01 is a scientifically differentiated, active IND-enabling Alzheimer's biologic with strong preclinical mechanism and data breadth, but its value depends on clinical proof of lower ARIA and meaningful cognitive benefit in a crowded anti-Aβ market.
