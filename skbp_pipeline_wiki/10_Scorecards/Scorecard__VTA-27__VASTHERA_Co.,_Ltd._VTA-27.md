## 2. Criterion Details

### Target Relevance

- Score: 3/3
- Evidence Type: E3_asset_specific_preclinical_or_technical_evidence
- Reason: Direct AD fit with mechanistically linked astrocyte/STAT3 neuroimmune biology.
- Evidence type reason: AD is an SKBP-interest indication and VTA-27 has asset-specific PRX/STAT3 neuroinflammation data, supported by independent astrocyte-STAT3/H2O2 AD biology.
- Why not higher: 3 is the maximum score.
- Uncertain points:
- Human disease-causal importance of PRX inactivation remains unproven prospectively.

### Competitive Landscape

- Score: 2/3
- Evidence Type: E3_asset_specific_preclinical_or_technical_evidence
- Reason: Direct PRX-mimetic competition appears sparse, but AD/neuroinflammation competition is substantial.
- Evidence type reason: VTA-27 has quantitative preclinical differentiation in a realistic AD entry space, but no head-to-head study or clinical differentiation versus current/advanced competitors.
- Why not higher: No asset-specific head-to-head comparison or clinical leading-position evidence; multiple later-stage alternatives exist.
- Uncertain points:
- No public same-PRX-mimetic AD competitor was verified; absence of a found competitor is not treated as proof of exclusivity.

### MoA Validity

- Score: 3/3
- Evidence Type: E3_asset_specific_preclinical_or_technical_evidence
- Reason: Direct functional validation links VTA-27 redox activity to STAT3/astrocyte modulation and neuroprotection.
- Evidence type reason: Asset-specific H2O2 catalytic activity and Aβ-induced pSTAT3 inhibition directly test the proposed PRX-mimetic mechanism, with in-vivo downstream pathology/cognition support.
- Why not higher: 3 is the maximum; human target engagement/PD has not yet been shown.
- Uncertain points:
- Deck reports pSTAT3 IC50 as 131.1 nM on slide 20 but 162 nM in the candidate profile on slide 27.

### Platform Attractiveness

- Score: 1/3
- Evidence Type: E3_asset_specific_preclinical_or_technical_evidence
- Reason: Reusable PRX-mimetic platform is credible, but comparative platform advantage is not yet demonstrated.
- Evidence type reason: Redoxizyme is a reusable PRX-mimetic platform and VTA-27 has quantitative technical activity, but no appropriate comparator demonstrates platform-level quantitative advantage.
- Why not higher: Score 2 requires quantitative advantage versus an appropriate comparator; no such platform-level comparison was verified.
- Uncertain points:
- Cross-asset quantitative comparability of Redoxizyme-derived compounds is not publicly established.

### Expansion Potential

- Score: 0/3
- Evidence Type: E1_company_claim_or_scientific_rationale_only
- Reason: PD is only a potential future expansion, not a confirmed asset-specific program.
- Evidence type reason: The deck mentions only 'potential expansion to PD'; no confirmed active VTA-27 PD program or asset-specific PD data was found.
- Why not higher: Score 1 requires a confirmed additional indication with biological rationale; future opportunity wording alone is excluded by the rubric.
- Uncertain points:
- A dedicated VTA-27 PD study or pipeline listing could change this score.

### Data Maturity

- Score: 3/3
- Evidence Type: E3_asset_specific_preclinical_or_technical_evidence
- Reason: Broad asset-specific preclinical package supports IND-enabling progression.
- Evidence type reason: Multiple complementary quantitative domains are available: in-vitro catalytic/STAT3 activity, in-vivo pathology/cognition, and IND-enabling safety/CMC/PK work.
- Why not higher: 3 is the maximum; no VTA-27 human data are available.
- Uncertain points:
- Key efficacy studies used i.p. dosing; independent blinded oral 5xFAD confirmation is still ongoing/planned in the deck.

### Marketability

- Score: 2/3
- Evidence Type: E2_indirect_or_class_level_evidence
- Reason: Calculated global obtainable peak sales are about 1,220.50 million USD.
- Evidence type reason: A transparent US-base screening calculation uses independent epidemiology and current anti-amyloid price benchmarks; no asset-specific external peak-sales forecast was found.
- Why not higher: The result is below 2,000 million USD and relies on screening assumptions rather than clinical VTA-27 uptake evidence.
- Uncertain points:
- Diagnosis rate is a proxy from MCI/dementia detection, not an AD-specific rate.
- Benchmark net price uses a 20% gross-to-net screening discount from the 2026 LEQEMBI IQLIK list price.
