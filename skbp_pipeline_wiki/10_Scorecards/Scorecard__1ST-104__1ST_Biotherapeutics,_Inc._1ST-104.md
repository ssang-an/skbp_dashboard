## 2. Criterion Details

### Target Relevance

- Score: 3/3
- Evidence Type: E3_asset_specific_preclinical_or_technical_evidence
- Reason: Strong PD fit with genetically validated LRRK2 biology and asset-specific dual-target activity.
- Evidence type reason: 1ST-104 is confirmed as an LRRK2/c-Abl Parkinson's program with direct asset-specific target pharmacology, supported by independent PD target biology.
- Why not higher: Score 3 is the maximum; clinical proof of benefit for the dual-target strategy is still absent.
- Uncertain points:
- Incremental clinical contribution of c-Abl inhibition is unproven.

### Competitive Landscape

- Score: 2/3
- Evidence Type: E3_asset_specific_preclinical_or_technical_evidence
- Reason: Quantitative differentiation exists, but several clinical LRRK2 competitors are ahead and recent efficacy failures increase risk.
- Evidence type reason: Internal quantitative pRab10/pS935 comparison supports differentiation versus a Type I comparator, while multiple clinical LRRK2 programs define a crowded field.
- Why not higher: No clinical or independently replicated head-to-head differentiation; multiple high-similarity programs are ahead.
- Uncertain points:
- Clinical relevance of pS935 preservation is not established.
- Exact entry timing versus Phase 2 competitors depends on candidate nomination and IND progression.

### MoA Validity

- Score: 3/3
- Evidence Type: E3_asset_specific_preclinical_or_technical_evidence
- Reason: Strong direct preclinical validation of intended dual-target pharmacology.
- Evidence type reason: Direct biochemical inhibition and mechanism-linked cellular biomarkers are demonstrated for both LRRK2 and c-Abl.
- Why not higher: Score 3 is the maximum; LRRK2 Type II binding itself has not yet been structurally confirmed for the assessed compounds.
- Uncertain points:
- LRRK2 cryo-EM structural confirmation was planned rather than completed in the reviewed deck.

### Platform Attractiveness

- Score: 1/3
- Evidence Type: E1_company_claim_or_scientific_rationale_only
- Reason: Reusable AI/medchem infrastructure is credible, but differentiated technical performance is not quantitatively demonstrated.
- Evidence type reason: A reusable AI-enabled drug discovery engine is disclosed and used, but quantitative technical superiority is not benchmarked.
- Why not higher: No platform-vs-comparator quantitative result on hit rate, cycle time, quality or cost.
- Uncertain points:
- Magnitude of AI contribution versus conventional medicinal chemistry is unknown.

### Data Maturity

- Evidence Type: E3_asset_specific_preclinical_or_technical_evidence
- Reason: Broad and quantitative lead-optimization package across several development questions.
- Evidence type reason: Multiple complementary quantitative domains include target pharmacology, cellular biomarkers, selectivity, ADME and CNS PK.
