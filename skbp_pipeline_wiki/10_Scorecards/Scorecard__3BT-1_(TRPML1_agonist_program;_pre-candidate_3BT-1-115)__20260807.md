## 2. Criterion Details

### Target Relevance

- Score: 3/3
- Evidence Type: E3_asset_specific_preclinical_or_technical_evidence
- Reason: Alzheimer's disease is a six-interest indication, and the assessed TRPML1 agonist program is directly tied to lysosomal/autophagy biology with independent AD/PD target validation.
- Evidence type reason: Alzheimer's disease is a six-interest indication, and the assessed TRPML1 agonist program is directly tied to lysosomal/autophagy biology with independent AD/PD target validation.
- Why not higher: 3 is the maximum score.
- Uncertain points:
- Theme/Cluster mapping to E/I Balance / Ion Channel is an operational SKBP mapping; the biological thesis is lysosomal proteostasis rather than classical synaptic excitation/inhibition.

### Competitive Landscape

- Score: 2/3
- Evidence Type: E3_asset_specific_preclinical_or_technical_evidence
- Reason: 3BT-1-115 has quantitative comparator data versus Leqembi, an LRRK2 inhibitor, a PINK1 activator and L-DOPA, but no disclosed head-to-head data versus the most similar TRPML1 agonists; LW-1017 entered Phase 1 in May 2026.
- Evidence type reason: 3BT-1-115 has quantitative comparator data versus Leqembi, an LRRK2 inhibitor, a PINK1 activator and L-DOPA, but no disclosed head-to-head data versus the most similar TRPML1 agonists; LW-1017 entered Phase 1 in May 2026.
- Why not higher: A leading position is not verified because at least one high-similarity competitor is already clinical and several same-target programs are IND-enabling; direct same-MoA comparative potency, free-brain exposure, efficacy and safety data are unavailable.
- Uncertain points:
- No reliable public source was found for current clinical status of every historical TRPML1 program mentioned in the company deck.
- Competitor indication disclosures differ, so similarity classification is conservative.

### MoA Validity

- Score: 3/3
- Evidence Type: E3_asset_specific_preclinical_or_technical_evidence
- Reason: The deck shows direct TRPML1 current activation by lysosomal patch clamp and a Cryo-EM binding structure for 3BT-1-115, plus downstream lysosomal, aggregate-clearance and inflammatory readouts; independent literature supports TRPML1 biology in AD and PD.
- Evidence type reason: The deck shows direct TRPML1 current activation by lysosomal patch clamp and a Cryo-EM binding structure for 3BT-1-115, plus downstream lysosomal, aggregate-clearance and inflammatory readouts; independent literature supports TRPML1 biology in AD and PD.
- Why not higher: 3 is the maximum score; however, no human target-engagement/PD data are available.
- Uncertain points:
- The asset-specific mechanistic dataset is company-generated and not verified in a peer-reviewed publication.
- Exposure-response translation from free brain concentration to TRPML1 activation/target engagement remains unresolved.

### Platform Attractiveness

- Score: 1/3
- Evidence Type: E1_company_claim_or_scientific_rationale_only
- Reason: AION is described as a reusable electrophysiology/CNS assay platform and is used commercially, but the materials do not show a quantitative platform-level advantage versus an appropriate competing technical system.
- Evidence type reason: AION is described as a reusable electrophysiology/CNS assay platform and is used commercially, but the materials do not show a quantitative platform-level advantage versus an appropriate competing technical system.
- Why not higher: Score 2 requires explicit quantitative platform-level technical superiority versus an appropriate comparator; asset efficacy data cannot be reused as proof of AION platform superiority.
- Uncertain points:
- CRO customer logos and revenue do not establish assay accuracy, throughput, reproducibility, predictive validity or cost/time advantage.

### Expansion Potential

- Score: 2/3
- Evidence Type: E3_asset_specific_preclinical_or_technical_evidence
- Reason: Beyond lead Alzheimer's disease, 3BT-1-115 has quantitative Parkinson's disease in-vivo efficacy readouts including dopamine PET-CT and motor tests.
- Evidence type reason: Beyond lead Alzheimer's disease, 3BT-1-115 has quantitative Parkinson's disease in-vivo efficacy readouts including dopamine PET-CT and motor tests.
- Why not higher: Score 3 requires at least two distinct additional indications, at least one active asset-specific program, with asset-specific quantitative efficacy/PD/biomarker data; ALS is mentioned at company/platform level but no 3BT-1-115 ALS dataset was verified.
- Uncertain points:
- It is not publicly confirmed whether the Parkinson's program uses the exact same development candidate that is now in IND-enabling work.

### Data Maturity

- Score: 3/3
- Evidence Type: E3_asset_specific_preclinical_or_technical_evidence
- Reason: The asset has multiple complementary quantitative preclinical domains: direct target activation/structure, cell functional assays, PK/BBB/ADME, AD and PD in-vivo efficacy, and non-GLP safety signals; current public reporting says IND-enabling work is underway.
- Evidence type reason: The asset has multiple complementary quantitative preclinical domains: direct target activation/structure, cell functional assays, PK/BBB/ADME, AD and PD in-vivo efficacy, and non-GLP safety signals; current public reporting says IND-enabling work is underway.
- Why not higher: 3 is the maximum score.
- Uncertain points:
- A 2026 exhibitor page still described lead optimization and expected Q2 candidate nomination, whereas a July 2026 article says IND-enabling work is underway; no regulatory filing source resolves the transition.
- Most quantitative data are from a company IR deck rather than independent peer-reviewed publication.

- Reason: An assumption-based US calculation for early AD yields US obtainable peak sales of 1474.12 million USD and screening-policy global peak sales of 2211.17 million USD, crossing the >=2000 threshold.
- Evidence type reason: An assumption-based US calculation for early AD yields US obtainable peak sales of 1474.12 million USD and screening-policy global peak sales of 2211.17 million USD, crossing the >=2000 threshold.
- Why not higher: 3 is the maximum score, but the result is scenario-based rather than forecast-backed; no reliable asset-specific external peak-sales forecast was found.
