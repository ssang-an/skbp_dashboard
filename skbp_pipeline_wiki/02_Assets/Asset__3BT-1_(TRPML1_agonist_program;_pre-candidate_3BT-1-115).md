## 2. One-line Summary

Scientifically differentiated and data-rich preclinical TRPML1 program with current IND-enabling activity, but clinical first-mover competition and exposure/target-engagement translation are the decisive diligence risks.

## 3. Why This Asset Matters

- Strategic relevance: Alzheimer's disease is a six-interest indication, and the assessed TRPML1 agonist program is directly tied to lysosomal/autophagy biology with independent AD/PD target validation.
- Scientific rationale: The deck shows direct TRPML1 current activation by lysosomal patch clamp and a Cryo-EM binding structure for 3BT-1-115, plus downstream lysosomal, aggregate-clearance and inflammatory readouts; independent literature supports TRPML1 biology in AD and PD.
- BD relevance: At the efficacy dose, does unbound brain exposure of the exact IND-enabling molecule sustain sufficient TRPML1 activation and in-vivo target engagement (e.g., TFEB/p62/lysosomal PD) with an adequate chronic safety margin relative to LW-1017/CSM-101/LBR-2950?

- Need full GLP tox/safety pharmacology/CMC status and planned IND/CTA jurisdiction/timing.
- Need exposure-response bridge: free-brain concentration at efficacy dose versus TRPML1 functional EC50/EC90 and in-vivo target-engagement markers.
- Need chronic systemic TRPML1 safety package, including inner-ear exposure/ABR and other lysosomal-homeostasis liabilities acknowledged in the company Q&A.
- Need direct head-to-head data versus LW-1017/CSM-101/LBR-2950 or a defensible cross-study benchmark.
