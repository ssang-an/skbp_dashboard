## 2. One-line Summary

ILM01 is a scientifically differentiated, active IND-enabling Alzheimer's biologic with strong preclinical mechanism and data breadth, but its value depends on clinical proof of lower ARIA and meaningful cognitive benefit in a crowded anti-Aβ market.

## 3. Why This Asset Matters

- Strategic relevance: Alzheimer's disease is an SKBP interest indication, and ILM01 directly links Aβ clearance to glial TAM-receptor neuroimmune biology.
- Scientific rationale: Asset-specific TAM engagement, Aβ uptake, cytokine modulation, and in vivo inflammatory-marker changes provide direct functional validation of the proposed mechanism.
- BD relevance: Can Illimis provide full study reports and independent replication showing that the final ILM01 candidate—not only the 2022 predecessor construct—maintains Aβ clearance and cognitive benefit with materially lower ARIA at clinically relevant exposure, together with completed NHP PK/TK, GLP toxicology, CMC readiness, and clear ILM01/Lilly rights?
